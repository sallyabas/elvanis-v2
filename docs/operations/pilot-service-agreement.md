## 6. What this output is and what it is not

**What it is:**  
A structured, evidence-based readiness assessment reviewed by an experienced product and AI advisor. Findings are grounded in evidence you submitted. Every finding has been personally accepted, edited, or rejected by Sally Abas before you see it.

**What it is not:**  
- Legal advice
- A compliance certification or regulatory approval
- A guarantee of regulatory compliance
- A substitute for qualified legal counsel on specific regulatory questions
- An audit conducted by a licensed auditor or regulated professional

The output of every Elvanis report is advisory. It is a starting point for action and for conversations with qualified professionals — not the end point.

**Regulatory framework currency:**  
Our regulatory frameworks (EU AI Act, UAE DIFC Regulation 10, Saudi SDAIA, GDPR, Saudi PDPL) are reviewed manually and dated. If a regulation changes significantly after your report is delivered, the report may not reflect that change. We recommend re-auditing if your regulatory environment changes materially.

**AI Reliability Audit specific limitation:**  
The PASS/FAIL classification in the AI Reliability Audit involves LLM judgment with a keyword-based safety net. It is not a deterministic rule. Bias-category findings carry an explicit "not checked by safety net" disclosure. These limitations are disclosed in your report.

**Single reviewer:**  
All reports are reviewed by Sally Abas personally. There is no second reviewer. This is a disclosed limitation of the current service.
